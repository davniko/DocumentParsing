Extract document-supported facts from the complete, page-ordered Bill of Lading or Sea Waybill.
Return exactly one compact JSON object conforming to the supplied sparse extraction schema.

Semantic rules:

- All pages form one document. Consolidate repeated facts; retain distinct rows in source order.
- Keep party and route roles distinct. Party `address` combines street, building and postal text;
  separately printed city and country belong in their own fields.
- Preserve printed locality wording and measurement units (`MT` = `metric_tonne`). Dates need
  their matching B/L declaration; volume needs a printed CBM/m³ value.
- Record distinct containers and semantic cargo groups in source order. Container size/type
  categories require printed equipment evidence; otherwise use the printed `typeDescription`.
- For each goods item, record the innermost counted package level, distinct additive rows and
  exact totals derivable from all printed components. Use `typeOfPackages` for uncategorized
  printed packaging.
- Under each goods item, put printed container membership and allocation rows in
  `splitGoodsPlacement` using a listed container's `equipmentIdentifier`. Include
  `packageQuantity` when supported; package totals and placement quantities may be independent.
- Use the schema's dangerous-goods categories. Keep subsidiary hazards, packing group and
  flashpoint separate.
