## Interpretation limits

- A foreign exporter can legitimately differ from the bill-of-lading shipper.
- A combined `SHIPPER/EXPORTER` heading does not prevent a separate customs exporter from appearing elsewhere in the same document.
- Country mismatch is a review signal, not a universal same-entity rule.
- The complete inventory deliberately favors recall; references in boilerplate or customs identifiers are included when the source literally says `exporter`.
