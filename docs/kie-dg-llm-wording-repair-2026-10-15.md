# DG commercial wording: root correction and dataset repair

Date: 2026-10-15. Scope: dangerous-goods wording, its repeated presentations,
description labels, source/template ownership, and synthetic coordinates.

Status: **completed and independently read back**. The actual training loader
accepts 7,600 training rows and 60 validation rows. All 413 registered templates compile.

Later follow-up: the [1,000-description refresh](kie-goods-description-refresh1000-2026-10-15.md)
updates ordinary/thermal wording in this same R26 directory. All DG rows repaired
here remain unchanged. The exact original R26 publication is preserved in that
refresh's backup; its independent file hashes should not be confused with the
later manifest containing both repairs. DG already shares the polished wording
prompt and now also inherits the clarified host-count/source-assembly guidance.

## Decision and diagnosis

DG commercial descriptions must be generated by the LLM, using the same polished
source-like wording guidance as ordinary goods. The old host-owned shortcut was
an implementation defect, not an agreed policy. It suppressed the product fields
for chemical and whole-unit DG scenarios, then injected the registry phrase.
For example, a 108-word source catalogue could become only `CYCLOPENTANOL`.

Sampled product identity and regulatory facts remain authoritative; commercial
prose does not have to reproduce a proper shipping name verbatim. The LLM may
generate compatible grades, formulations' existing physical form, product details
and vehicle wording. It must not resample the substance, propulsion or hazard
tuple. Optional DG labels remain conditional on the source's printed fields.

The [improvement ledger](mpci-bl-proposed-improvements-2026-10-06.md) records this
as a priority correction. The earlier thermal/DG prompt report now explicitly
marks its host-only DG controls as historical evidence, not approval of that policy.

## What changed upstream

- `curated_campaign.py`: DG chemicals and DG vehicles now retain product fields in
  the native wording request. DG source-description assemblies must reproduce their
  approved source blocks, preventing stale Marks/reference material from entering
  the model's assembly instructions. Existing non-DG whole-unit behavior is unchanged.
- `curated_dg_wording.py`: supplies sampled identity, regulatory compatibility and
  declared package counts as context. Actual physical-plan counts take precedence
  when a plan is supplied. Context is not extra text to insert into the description.
- `curated_auxiliary.py` and `curated_ownership.py`: explicit `dg_fact:
  properShippingName` bindings separate independently printed regulatory names from
  the full generated commercial description. Prefixes such as `PSN: ` are retained.
  Missing identity or mismatched UN facts fail; no registry-name fallback is hidden.
- `curated_wording.py`: review instructions distinguish source presentation from
  the newly sampled commodity. The existing accounting guard now catches compact
  mass totals such as `TOTAL 26MT` and `TOTAL:26MT`. Obvious independently owned DG declarations cannot leak into
  generated product prose. Intrinsic fills, capacities and product specifications
  remain allowed; no payload-limit or arbitrary packing-arithmetic classifier was added.

DG wording uses the existing joint PydanticAI native structured-output call and
its existing bounded validation/correction flow. No extra runtime classifier,
word-count quota, separate DG lexical agent, or stronger model was introduced.

## Live experiments and findings

All generation uses OpenRouter `z-ai/glm-5.3-flash`, low reasoning, native
PydanticAI structured outputs; OCR/source examples are plain text.

- Initial contained trial: 12 templates × 3 variations = 36 LLM-generated outputs
  in 12 native batches, followed by exact renderer/label/coordinate replay.
- Integrated trial: another 36 variations across those 12 templates. All 36 renders
  preserve unrelated targets and independently match their description-block labels.
- Full affected-source renderer test: **81 renders, three per each of27 templates**.
  Regulatory names and commercial repeats use their respective owners correctly.
- Final focused native smoke:12 new variations across four sources, specifically
  including the corrected petrol-vehicle assembly, order/PSN repetition, solid DG
  preparation and packing-bearing descriptions. The corrected vehicle template
  produces ordinary vehicle names/specifications without inventing another VIN.

Initial DG prose did expose concrete defects. These were not passed through:
15 documents received targeted native wording corrections for mismatched declared
package counts, invented mass totals, one changed solid preparation, and one
internally inconsistent vehicle specification. An extra unrequested PG caption
was removed from one corrected wording.32 vehicle wordings had a VIN tail induced
by the stale assembly; only that tail was removed, retaining their generated prose.
Each correction has before/after evidence and is followed by complete render/label
and positional revalidation. Original proposals and charged/failed attempts remain recorded.

Short identity-only examples can still produce the same chemical name. That is
now an observed LLM result, not unconditional host substitution. This repair does
not promise artificial uniqueness for every one-word product or chemical synonym.

### Semantic review and false positives

449 documents received an additional scoped model review. Six cases whose review
requests repeatedly failed upstream were directly read by the primary assistant,
with individual notes and final-row hashes. This is **not** presented as455
successful API-review responses. The inspection covers changed commercial wording,
full description-label projection, repeats and preserved shipment facts; it is not
a new reannotation of unrelated document fields or carrier boilerplate.

All 28 model findings were explicitly adjudicated. Rejected false positives include:

- Treating the original source commodity as the newly sampled shipment identity.
- Mistaking deliberately repeated PSN/chemical-name occurrences for duplicated output.
- Missing unchanged host-rendered model passages that were outside the edited-value list.
- Multiplying catalogue/per-unit details into an undeclared shipment grand total.
- Incorrectly recalling UN mappings: the pinned HMT agrees with the samples.
  [NOAA CAMEO confirms UN1918 as cumene/isopropylbenzene](https://cameochemicals.noaa.gov/report?key=UN1918).

The approval criteria remain extraction consistency and plausible sampled DG
identity, not a new legal-shipping certification or an exact HS-to-product mapping.

## Dataset publication and validation

The repaired dataset is published as
`data/curated/mpci-bl-real600-synthetic7000-dg-llm-wording-r26-positions-v1`.
It preserves the 7,000 synthetic training membership, 600 real rows, 60 validation
rows, previous-UAE sidecar and selected old-UAE mirror. Mirrors receive identical
repairs. Training targets remain the **direct document object**, without a
`schemaVersion`/`documentPatch` wrapper.

All 455 DG wordings were regenerated and validated. 264 unique records materially
change; the remaining 191 naturally retain their earlier wording and anchors.
The training file changes 261 rows, the complete old-UAE sidecar 36, and its selected
mirror 33. Real/validation files and all non-DG rows remain byte-identical.

Publication is staged and manifest-last. A per-document receipt proves:

1. Exact original/current source ownership and disjoint, replayable edits.
2. Description labels projected from approved rendered blocks; only description
   target paths change. UN/class/PG/subsidiary/flash, HS formatting, package facts,
   placements, routes, parties, dates and identifiers remain unchanged.
3. All non-owned input bytes survive, including repaired phones, container-ID
   presentations, private-reference anonymization and unrelated footers.
4. Physical-line-preserving edits retain every current coordinate suffix exactly.
   Changed line spans use measured source boxes, explicit edit proofs and the same
   transfer/reflow/augmentation engine as production. Missing geometry remains
   explicitly missing; text is never dropped to fit.
5. Coordinate bounds, page ownership and complete positioned/raw text preservation.
6. Source/template snapshots, generated responses and correction provenance remain
   independently inspectable; historical training files are not overwritten.

382 affected documents preserve all current line anchors exactly; 73 require
measured-source reflow. Final coverage is 45,026/54,391 content lines (82.78%),
versus 45,095/54,261 (83.11%) before the wording expansion. The small coverage
change is explicit: longer/differently wrapped passages cannot always inherit a
source point. All generated coordinates pass bounds/ownership checks; no text or
label is dropped to obtain a coordinate. Unpositioned lines retain the explicit delimiter.

The evidence directory is
[dg-llm-wording-repair-20261015](analysis/dg-llm-wording-repair-20261015/).
Key receipts: `publication.json`, `independent-readback.json`,
`fresh-render-validation.json`, `repair-summary.json`,
`semantic-review/adjudication.json`, and `certification-smoke/summary.json`.
The [final source/render/label inspection pairs](analysis/dg-llm-wording-repair-20261015/FINAL_SOURCES_VARIATIONS.md)

## Tests and measured impact

The complete curated suite plus migration falsification controls passes
**1,717 tests**. Ruff and whitespace checks pass. Negative controls cover stale
source assemblies, stale quotes, duplicate edits, added pages, wrong UN facts,
unsupported identity recipes, invented compact mass totals, and unwanted DG
declarations. Intrinsic per-unit fills and capacity/model strings remain allowed.

The complete 455-document surgical replay, including unchanged-line anchor
preservation and measured reflow where needed, takes about 22 seconds on this host.
The 81 renderer controls take about 11.5 seconds. Exact final counts, geometry
coverage, cost accounting and CPU/memory measurements are in the machine receipts.
Request-construction timings are explicitly a host-only/full-DG-request ablation,
not a claim of end-to-end model speedup. Normal generation still uses its existing
joint call; one-off dataset repair/review calls are separately accounted.

The request ablation measures 243.30µs versus 523.18µs, an additional 279.89µs per
DG request: negligible compared with the existing network call, with no extra
runtime generation call. Independent dataset/registry/loader readback takes 48.71s,
peaking at 830,472KiB RSS (about 811MiB). These are CPU-only checks.

Provider429/502 responses were retained and explicitly resumed, never counted
as accepted samples. Reported charges and reserved-unknown upper bounds are kept
separate in the final call ledger. No GPU run or training was launched.
Confirmed provider-reported charges for this repair and its trials total
$0.19556. The 20 unknown-status failed requests retain a conservative additional
reservation bound of $0.24510; this is not presented as a confirmed charge.
