## Confirmed cause and repair strategy

The retained service contract belongs to a historical accepted
`forwardingAndExportReferences` owner typed `other_text`. The extraction field
was dropped, but its source-only privacy ownership/generator was never added.
The existing typed-identifier guard therefore never saw that owner. The
existing source-format formatter already supports the necessary repair; it
does not require a new identifier generator, country-specific logic or broader
extraction targets.

Each private identifier will receive reviewed typed ownership and the existing
source-format recipe. Composite owners must isolate only the private code,
preserving printed dates and captions. Repeated occurrences must share the same
synthetic identity where they refer to the same original identifier. Current
values that are already anonymized are not resampled merely because an old
historical binding still mentions the private source. Original sources and
historical receipts remain immutable evidence.

The compiler instruction's exclusion of already-anchored material also needs a
bounded clarification: an old accepted anchor is not a privacy exemption when
its target is dropped during rebasing. Private-code classification must not be
replaced with a whole-text regex or a blanket rule that dates and public carrier
names are private identifiers.

completed publisher/plan/receipts remain historical evidence; they are not
rewritten to conceal this check. Future readiness must exercise the actual
training loader before a training launch, rather than accepting an in-memory
