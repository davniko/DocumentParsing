# UAE-end synthesis expansion: 2,500 new samples

Date: 2026-10-11. This report records the requested additional synthesis,
coverage investigation, actual generation and acceptance evidence. No model
training is launched by this work.

**Completed:** all 2,500 additions are accepted, positioned, published and
independently byte-verified. The saved dataset contains 5,000 synthetic training
records, 600 preserved real training records and 60 preserved validation records.

## Scope and reproducible configuration

The inherited dataset has 600 real training records, 2,500 synthetic training
records and 60 real validation records. This campaign adds exactly 2,500
synthetic records. The expanded mixture is therefore 5,600 training records
(600 real + 5,000 synthetic), with the same 60 validation records.

All **new** samples must have UNITED ARAB EMIRATES import/end geography, including
independently modeled delivery and final-destination nodes. This is not a
retroactive change to the inherited 2,500 synthetic records. Origins,
transshipment nodes and independent third-country offices remain variable.

Two configurations retain the registry's two explicitly designated source
authorities:

- [Reviewed R16: 338 sources, 2,367 samples](../configs/synthesis/mpci_bl_curated_v7_uae2500_r16.yaml).
- [Corrected frozen authority: 20 sources, 133 samples](../configs/synthesis/mpci_bl_curated_v7_uae2500_authority.yaml).

The seed is `202610111`; per-source totals range from 3 to 33. The
`variants_per_source: 2` setting is the request batch size, not a uniform
per-source descendant quota. Generation uses the established production
PydanticAI/native-output GLM-5.3-Flash path, low reasoning, bounded concurrency
and all-attempt provider accounting. Failed requests are never treated as
accepted labels or silently replaced with source values.

## Why these distributions

The latest saved-output audit identifies strong container-membership recovery,
but weaker package-quantity restraint, some equipment-category omissions and
incomplete descriptions. Equal descendants per template would not directly
address those weaknesses. The campaign therefore weights source capabilities
and narrowly certified category domains.

## Deliberately uncovered shapes and excluded sources

The current admitted registry cannot honestly supply genuine product continuations
across pages, mixed known/unknown allocation quantities, known container type with
missing length, or known 20HC equipment. Nor do its certified donor/identity
contracts provide usable IBC, multi-wall/plain paper bag, unpackaged-unit,
jumbo-bag, coil or rare-powder domains. Vocabulary presence alone is not complete
sampling support. These are retained as explicit future coverage work, not
invented into the new labels.

Two of the 360 registered sources are excluded from this campaign:

- `9ad49fcc`: a fixed phone-prefix ownership issue can produce `++` on new
  international numbers. Nine inherited descendants remain historical and
  unchanged; this campaign does not claim to have freshly repaired them.
- `00a165a2`: fixed BANHA inland handling lacks a source-owned final/delivery
  locality, so changing only the discharge port would not establish UAE end
  geography.

The initial 70 exact-span, campaign-local auxiliary recipes in 31 sources neutralize
explicit former Egypt-end handling/customs clauses. They do not mutate the
shared template registry, rewrite unrelated conditional legal flavor or collapse
independent office roles. All 93 applicable plan probes and their 93 corruption
controls passed before generation. The further source-owned auxiliary corrections
discovered by complete-pair review are described below and included in the final
configuration and independent audit hashes.

## Acceptance procedure

1. Verify protected baseline, registry, source authorities, source owners,
   auxiliary recipes, registries, schema and configuration pins.
2. Execute all 2,500 exact shipment plans and production preflights before
   paying for wording. Check every modeled terminal node, not just discharge.
3. Generate wording and contacts; preserve actual native request/response
   receipts, retries and cost. Correct only demonstrated bad values, retaining
   sound fields and original evidence.
4. Independently replay source-owner edits and approved description boundaries;
   check all targets, declared-unit allocations and numeric row ownership.
5. Obtain a model review of every complete rendered-text/target pair. Adjudicate
   every finding against literal current OCR and policy, bound to current
   candidate and finding hashes. Changed candidates require fresh review.
6. Publish plain samples, then anchor-only and reflowed positioned variants.
   Independently verify source occurrence/page lineage, geometry, order, bounds,
   no lost anchors and exact text/target preservation. Inspect diagnostic plots.
7. Run the independent final offline audit, publish the expanded mixture only
   after a matching successful audit, then independently verify the published
   bytes. Preserve inherited rows and validation literally.

The independent checker also binds reviews to actual model-call system/user
messages, output schema, provider response identity, parsed output and complete
retry ledger. A manually written review summary cannot substitute for a call.
Negative controls deliberately corrupt this evidence, geography, schemas,
quantities, geometry, publication contents and preserved baseline bytes.

## Completed generation, review and corrections

Exactly **2,500/2,500** new samples are generated, rendered and accepted by the
plain-publication contract: 2,367 from the R16 authority and 133 from the corrected
frozen authority. No unresolved finding, repair-required finding, render failure
or held document remains in either new cohort. Source quotas are nonuniform,
3–33 per source, and all 358 selected sources are represented. The 120 earlier
template-admission pilot descendants are not included as training additions.

Every final rendered-text/target pair has an actual saved model review. That is
different from saying a human personally opened all 2,500 documents. Reviews
use the complete rendered text and full target, alongside sampled facts and
source-owned identity/packing context. Every flagged finding was individually
adjudicated against the current sample, policy and literal OCR evidence. A changed
candidate or review cannot inherit the old decision. The final current reviews
contain **528 R16 + 59 authority = 587** findings, all explicitly rejected after
the demonstrated defects below were repaired and the affected families freshly
reviewed. This final rejection count does not mean that the original generation
contained no defects, or that review findings were automatically waived.

The distinction between true defects and non-objectives remained important:
private commercial offices, source-owned manufacturer/Marks text, retail
capacities, exact HS/product ontology, product ratings and per-unit payload
arithmetic do not by themselves make an extraction label wrong. Incorrect party
ownership, contradictory printed equipment, cold-chain meaning, missing labels
for standalone shipment totals and generation-control text do.
